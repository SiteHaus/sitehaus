| F-062 | privacy     | medium   | sitehaus-commerce                                | `sitehaus-commerce:packages/database/src/customers.ts`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | `customers.notes` and `customers.tags` are **unbounded free-text fields a store owner can type anything into** — health conditions, complaints, disputes, whatever they please. A postal address is predictable and bounded; a notes field is an open-ended PII sink with no classification, no retention, and no way to reason about what is in it. The table currently has **zero rows**, which makes now the cheapest possible moment to constrain it (character limit, guidance copy, retention, or drop the fields). Riskier than the addresses everyone worries about.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
